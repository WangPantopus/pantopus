Reschedule / Reassign Sheet

A host or member moves a booking to a new time and/or member, pulling live composed availability. A tall bottom sheet over a dimmed Booking Detail: the current slot shown struck-through with a down-arrow to an empty "New time" target, then the shared availability picker reused from Support Trains — a horizontal weekday strip and full-width stacked slot-row buttons (never a dense grid, so it works at the largest dynamic type). A tappable timezone chip sits above the grid; where Home/Business compose members an explainer notes availability comes from each member, and Business reassign adds a member-picker row. A host-only authority toggle switches between "Propose to invitee" and "Reschedule now"; the sticky CTA reflects it.
