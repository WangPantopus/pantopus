Booking Search & Filter

Narrow the inbox by date range, status, owner-context, event-type, and invitee/text. A bottom sheet reusing the shared FilterSheetShell: a "Filter bookings" title with a "Clear all" text action (disabled until something is set), a search field at the top, then stacked filter sections — each an overline plus a wrap of pill chips: Status, Owner context (the active scope pre-selected, chips tinted with their identity colors), Event type, and Date range (Custom reveals two date inputs). Selected chips fill with the relevant tint; unselected are white with a 1px border. Above the CTA, a removable active-filter summary row appears when any are set. The sticky primary CTA shows the live result count — "Show 12 bookings" — and disables to "No matches" at zero.
