Pantopus · Calendarly · A — Set up & home base

Scheduling onboarding — Home & Business

Two first-run wizards parallel to the Personal setup-booking-link wizard, built for the materially harder Home and Business zero-to-value paths. Both reuse A12.11's StepRail (numbered discs, check-on-done, 2px connectors, "step X of N" overline), 2-col tile pickers, and the member-invite branch — recolored to their pillar. The hard part each solves: availability is composed from members' personal hours, so nobody sets times twice. That explainer is locked on every step where members combine.

Home

Family scheduling — 3 steps

Members → how times combine → share. Family scheduling uses everyone's own hours; the host picks who's bookable and whether times intersect (collective) or pool (round-robin).

HOME · STEP 1 · MEMBERS
Choose who's scheduled
A verified-household multi-select reused from the Support Train invite step: avatar + name + a 32×18 iOS toggle (fill = home green) per member, plus a dashed "Invite someone" row.
HOME · STEP 2 · COLLECTIVE
How should times combine?
2-col mode picker (1.5px active border) with a diagram glyph each: Collective = the overlap of everyone. Below it, the locked composed-availability explainer + shared-timezone confirm chip.
HOME · STEP 2 · ROUND-ROBIN
Whoever's free gets it
Round-robin selected: the union of members, plus an assignment-rule list (balanced vs priority) that only appears in this mode. Explainer stays locked.
HOME · STEP 3 · SUCCESS
Your family link is live
A18 success hero in home green: the live family booking link in a copy card, "Share link" primary and a "Members" ghost. StepRail reads fully complete.
Business

Business booking — 4 steps

Claim link → first service → seat the team → confirm mode. Booking times compose from each seated teammate's own availability; the owner chooses auto-confirm or approve-each.

BIZ · STEP 1 · CLAIM
Claim your business link
Monospace handle prefilled pantopus.com/book/acme-co with a live availability check returning a green "Available" chip. Continue is enabled once the slug resolves.
BIZ · STEP 1 · CONFLICT
That link is taken
Error border + alert glyph, inline message, and three monospace suggestion chips for one-tap fixes. Continue stays disabled until the slug resolves.
BIZ · STEP 2 · SERVICE
Add your first service
A 2-col service tile picker (Consultation / Quote visit / Site survey / Service call) with duration + price fields below. "Use defaults" skips in one tap.
BIZ · STEP 3 · TEAM
Seat your team
Team rows with role chips (Owner / Stylist / Front desk) and a per-seat toggle, a "3 of 5 seats used" counter, an "Invite teammate" row, and the locked composed-availability explainer.
BIZ · STEP 4 · CONFIRM
Auto-confirm or approve?
A segmented mode picker ("Auto-confirm bookings" vs "I approve each one"), approve selected, with an explainer: requests queue and the slot is held 24h until you confirm.
BIZ · SUCCESS
Acme Co. is taking bookings
A18 success hero in business violet: the live booking link, "Share link" primary and "Add service" ghost. Voice confirms "You approve each booking before it's confirmed."
