Waitlist Join & Management

The cross-event capacity-recovery surface: an invitee joins a waitlist when an event type or slot is full, and the host sees and promotes waitlisted people when space opens. Two coordinated surfaces. The invitee join (a host-branded bottom sheet, CloseTrainSheet shell) shows a "Fully booked" line with the requested window, a "Join the waitlist and we'll text you the moment a spot opens" body, an auto-detected timezone chip, a name/contact field for guests, and a primary "Join waitlist" — submitting swaps to an A18 joined-confirmation with the queue position. The host waitlist management (a section inside Booking Detail / Roster, ListOfRows + ManageTrain capacity header) shows a capacity strip over waitlisted rows, each with a "Promote to seat" button enabled only when a seat is open.
