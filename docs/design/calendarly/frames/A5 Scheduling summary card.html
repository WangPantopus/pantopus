Scheduling summary card

An at-a-glance "X bookings this month" pulse embedded at the top of the Scheduling Hub — not its own screen. Extends the Me.html stats card: one white card holding a row of metric cells split by 1px hairline dividers, with a period chip group, a flat sparkline in the pillar color, and a "See insights" link into the full dashboard. The card keys its accent and headline numbers off the active owner's pillar (Personal sky, Business violet) and rebinds its labels per owner. Powered by getBusinessInsights('30d').
