Scheduling hub

One owner-polymorphic front door that ties Personal, Home, and Business scheduling together. Extends Me.html — the identity switcher pill row re-scopes the entire screen and the accent color keys off the active pillar — but the body is a scheduling dashboard, not a profile. The durable booking link is the hero (copy / QR / share), a master toggle opens or pauses new bookings, a Today & upcoming agenda reuses the Home calendar row vocabulary, and A14.3 chevron rows route to event types, availability, connected calendars, and bookings.

FRAME 1 · DEFAULT · PERSONAL
Active, accepting bookings
Sky-blue Personal pill filled. Booking-link card with a live preview of the public page, the monospace handle, QR, and Copy / Share ghost buttons. Master toggle on. Today & upcoming agenda with one pending approval. Manage rows count current state; pinned "Share booking link" footer.
FRAME 2 · EMPTY / FIRST-RUN
Set up your booking link
Sections collapse to a single amber setup banner with one primary CTA into the wizard. The unconfigured Manage rows show faintly as dashed "Not set up" placeholders so the destination is legible.
FRAME 3 · LOADING SKELETON
Shimmer placeholders
Top bar stays solid; everything below shimmers in place — pill row, booking-link card, agenda rows, and the Manage group — mirroring the populated layout. Never a "Loading…" string.
FRAME 4 · PAUSED
Bookings turned off
Toggle card is replaced by an amber banner with a Resume pill; the link preview dims to a "Paused" state. Existing bookings stay on the calendar, and the footer CTA swaps to "Resume bookings".
FRAME 5 · HOME · COMPOSED
Times come from each member
Home-green accent. A composed-availability explainer line plus a member-avatar stack sits above the link card; the handle becomes the household. Agenda rows carry a cross-owner glyph naming whose booking it is.
FRAME 6 · PERMISSION-GATED
Member, view-only
For members without calendar.edit: an info banner up top, no toggle (a read-only "managed by owner" status instead), no Share, and Manage rows lose their chevrons. Copy link stays available for viewing.
