Cancel & Refund Sheet

Cancel a confirmed booking, capture a reason, and issue the policy-correct refund or restore a package credit. A destructive bottom sheet over a dimmed Booking Detail (ManageTrain close pattern): a header asking "Cancel this booking?" with a one-line summary, single-select reason chips (Changed plans / Emergency / Found someone else / Other), an optional note, and — when paid via Stripe Connect — a Refund section of money rows in tabular-nums with a Full / Partial / Per policy preset and a policy explainer. When a package credit was redeemed, a "Restore 1 session credit" switch appears instead. A full-width destructive confirm CTA reads "Cancel booking" or "Cancel & refund $40". Conditional rows appear only when relevant.
