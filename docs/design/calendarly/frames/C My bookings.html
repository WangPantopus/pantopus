My bookings

A signed-in app user's list of the bookings they made across other people's hosts — the booker-side counterpart to the host bookings-inbox (which they do not see here), and distinct from my-packages-credits. It lives at /me/bookings under You/Me, so it carries the tab bar. It mirrors List of Rows 1:1: a centered "My bookings" title, an Upcoming / Past segmented control, and tappable booking rows grouped under thin date overlines. Each row leads with the host's avatar and a small pillar dot (sky / green / violet — you book across all three), with an A09 status pill and a chevron into Manage Your Booking. Neutral list chrome.
