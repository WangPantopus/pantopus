Review & Confirm / Checkout

The single final summary before an invitee's booking is confirmed — at /book/[slug]/review — that conditionally absorbs payment and package-credit redemption. The who/what/when/where summary card mirrors Form.html's rhythm; the price block reuses A09.4 Invoice's fees table, hero total in primary-600, and identity-tinted dots; the saved-card row matches A14.6 Payments. Card entry is the native Stripe PaymentSheet behind a "Payment method" button — never a hand-drawn card form. We never mark paid client-side: the copy says we confirm once payment clears. The host's Personal-sky pillar accents the active CTA and the price hero.
