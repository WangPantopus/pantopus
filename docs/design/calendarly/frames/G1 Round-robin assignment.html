Round-Robin Assignment Sheet

The owner picks which team seats can take round-robin bookings and sets fairness. A Business-pillar bottom sheet off the Service Editor's "Anyone / Specific members" mode: a rule selector of three full-width radio-cards (Balanced / Priority order / Strict round-robin), then a roster of businessSeats rows (A10.7 vocabulary) — checkbox, avatar, name, "Uses personal availability" caption, and a weight stepper (Balanced) or drag handle (Priority order). A live blurb explains the rotation, and a sticky primary Done confirms. Violet accent on every active state; never recolored to sky.
