Double-Book Warning

Warn a host before confirming a manual or blocked event that overlaps an existing booking or another owner-context commitment. A centered confirm modal (WizardCloseConfirm DNA): a white card over a dimmed view, a 40px warning disc with a calendar-clock glyph, a sentence-case title "This time overlaps", and a one-line conflict summary, then a compact linked card for the conflicting booking that's tappable as "View the conflict". For Home, a line names whose personal availability conflicts. Two outcomes drive two frames: soft-overlap allows a permitted human override (Cancel + Book anyway); hard-conflict replaces the primary with a disabled "Can't book — member unavailable" lock state, leaving Cancel and a "Pick another member" link, with the disc shifted to error.
