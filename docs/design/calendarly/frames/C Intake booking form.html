Intake / Booking details form

The full-screen step after an invitee picks a time on the slot picker — the tail of the public web /book/[slug] flow at /book/[slug]/details, also reachable in-app post-slot-select. It extends the web GuestSignupModal form step into a real intake surface and mirrors Form.html exactly: 44px inputs with 8px radius, 1px borders, section overlines, the validation vocabulary, and a sticky-bottom primary CTA. A non-editable booking summary header holds the event type, host, date, time, and timezone, with a thin slot-hold countdown beneath. The accent borrows the host's pillar — Personal sky here — for focus rings and the hold chip.
