Booking link

The owner's single screen for managing their public booking page — the inverse of the public invitee flow. A long block-editor that mirrors A13 Edit Business Page's grouped sections and A14's settings toggle rows: a status switch, an editable public slug with a live availability check, page header fields, per-service visibility toggles, intro/confirmation copy, intake + payments links, a Listed / Link-only control, and copy / share / QR footer actions over a sticky save bar. Owner-polymorphic — the header pill and section overlines follow the active pillar (Personal sky, Business violet); the availability check, toggles, and CTAs stay sky. Conflicts ride a semantic error border, never a flood-fill or left accent.
