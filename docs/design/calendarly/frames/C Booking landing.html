Booking landing

The public page an invitee lands on at /book/[slug] — reached on the web, in-app via the pantopus://book/:slug deeplink, from the host's public profile "Book time", and from a QR, email, or SMS. Auth is optional. It mirrors the A21 persona/local profile header and A10.9 Support train's public event-type list: a pillar-banner header with the host's avatar and a verified green check, an Open-in-app banner, then a list of bookable event types. The banner, headline, and chips follow the host's pillar (Personal sky / Home green / Business violet); booking actions stay product sky.
