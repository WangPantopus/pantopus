Availability schedule editor

The atomic source of truth for the "home & business compose personal availability" model. One row per weekday with a left on/off toggle and, when on, one or more real labeled time-range buttons plus a "+ Add a block" affordance; a per-row "Copy to other days" clones hours. Above the grid sit a name field and a timezone card (auto-detected chip + "Lock to my timezone"); below it, link-out rows for date overrides and booking limits, and a sticky save bar. Mirrors the Support Train weekday grid and Form.html chrome. Personal sky tints only the identity pill and overlines — every control stays product blue.
