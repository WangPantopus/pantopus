Booking Detail

The single authoritative screen one booking lives on, host- and member-side. Mirrors the Transactional / Content Detail shell: top bar with back chevron + overflow kebab and a status pill in the trailing slot; a header block with event-type name, date·time·duration and an owner-context identity strip; stacked white SectionCards (requester with verification disc + message shortcut, assigned-member chip on Business, location module, collapsed intake accordion, and a lifecycle timeline with success checks); and a sticky bottom CTA dock that clears the tab bar — pending shows ghost Decline + primary Approve, confirmed shows Reschedule + Message, with everything else behind the overflow. A conflict surfaces as an amber banner above the dock.
