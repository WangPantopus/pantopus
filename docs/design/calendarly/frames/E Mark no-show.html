Mark No-Show

A quick confirmation that an invitee or host didn't attend, flipping the booking to a no-show terminal state. A compact centered confirmation-dialog (same DNA as the EventDetail delete dialog) — a small white card capped near 280px over a dimmed Booking Detail, not a tall sheet. A 40px error-tinted disc with a user-x glyph, a sentence-case title "Mark as no-show?", and a calm body line. For a group booking the body swaps for a multi-select who-no-showed roster with checkboxes and an optional note. Two side-by-side buttons: ghost "Keep open" left, solid error "Mark no-show" right. Only renders after the booking's start time; on confirm it feeds the post-meeting follow-up state.
