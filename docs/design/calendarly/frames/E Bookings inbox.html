Bookings Inbox

One owner-polymorphic home base where a host or member sees every booking across Personal, Home and Business, organized by lifecycle. Mirrors the A08 tabbed-list-with-status-chips archetype exactly as Support trains and My bids do: top bar with back chevron + centered "Bookings" title + search and filter icons; a horizontally-scrolling scope pill row (All / Personal / Home:Riverside / Business:Acme), the active pill filled in that scope's identity color; then a segmented control (Upcoming / Pending / Past / Cancelled) with a warning-tinted count badge on Pending. Rows are white cards — no left accent — with a verified invitee avatar, event type, day·date·time·timezone, status chip, an owner-context glyph and (on Business) an assigned-member chip, plus an overflow kebab. A "Share booking link" extended FAB sits bottom-right.
