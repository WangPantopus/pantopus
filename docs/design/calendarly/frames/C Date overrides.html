Date overrides & holidays

A bottom sheet over the dimmed Availability editor. A compact month calendar picks a date or drags a range; under it a segmented choice — Unavailable or Custom hours — sets what that date does, with a "Block a date range" link for vacations. Existing overrides list as white-card rows with a delete, and a Holiday sets card blocks recognized holidays in bulk. Mirrors A14.8 Vacation hold for the date-block mechanic, List of Rows for the overrides list, and Form.html for the per-date custom-hours field group. Personal sky tints only the sheet overline — every control stays product blue.
