Approve / Decline Request Sheet

One decision sheet a host uses to confirm or reject a pending booking. Mirrors the ManageTrain closing-confirmation bottom sheet: a rounded-top sheet with a grab handle rising over a dimmed Booking Detail. Top: a compact requester summary with verification disc, the requested slot as a strong line (day, date, time, timezone), and a collapsed intake-answers preview. If the slot overlaps a confirmed booking, an amber banner with a View conflict link appears before the actions. An optional note-to-requester ghost input, then primary Approve and a ghost-destructive Decline. Tapping Decline progressively reveals reason chips (Time doesn't work / Fully booked / Not a fit / Other) and a Propose another time link. Only renders for approval-required event types.
