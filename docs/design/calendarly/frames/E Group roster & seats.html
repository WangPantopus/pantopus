Group Event Roster & Seats

Manage the attendee roster for a group/multi-seat booking: capacity vs filled, waitlist, and per-attendee actions. Top bar: back chevron, centered "Roster", overflow. A capacity header reuses the Support Trains SlotPreview / ManageTrain stat machinery — a "12 of 16 seats filled" card with a fill bar (filled portion in the owner-context identity color, clamping to grayscale when full) plus a Confirmed / Pending / Waitlisted stat strip. Then a List-of-Rows avatar+kebab list of attendee rows with status chips and per-row actions, a Waitlist section with primary-tinted "Promote to seat" buttons (disabled when full, with a caption), host-only Add attendee + Adjust capacity controls, and a "Message all attendees" extended FAB.
