Who's free · Household availability

The one place a bespoke visualization earns its keep — a glanceable heat grid of who is free when, composed from each member's personal availability plus home events. Member rows down the left, time blocks across the top; cells read free (green) / busy / tentative / off-hours, with opted-out members shown as unknown. A legend and member filter chips sit alongside, and tapping a free block opens a small popover to Find a time or Add event. Read-only.
