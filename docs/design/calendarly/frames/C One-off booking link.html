One-off link generator

A compact config sheet that mints a single-use or time-boxed booking link for one specific invitee — reusing the token-invite infra (crypto-random id, SHA-256, expiry) behind an iOS share sheet. It opens from the Bookings Inbox FAB, a booking's follow-up, the Messages composer, and pillar booking settings. Mirrors A12.11 Start a Support Train's compact config and A13 Share Home's generated-link block. The identity chip follows the host's pillar (Personal sky / Business violet); Generate, Copy, chips, and toggles all stay product sky.
