Pantopus · Calendarly · Token-authed booking management

Manage your booking

The single surface a signed-out invitee lands on from a confirmation or reminder email — token-authed at /book/manage/:token, also via the pantopus://book/manage/:token deeplink and the in-app booking list. It reuses TokenAccept's validation/routing shell for the token states and mirrors the A09.4 Invoice summary-card layout for the booking body, with an A18 status badge at the top. Reschedule opens the shared slot grid; Cancel opens the shared Cancel & Refund sheet. Host pillar accents on a neutral chrome.
