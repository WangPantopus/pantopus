Scheduling notifications

Not a standalone screen — the new "Scheduling & bookings" category card that slots into the existing A14.5 Notifications matrix, matched 1:1. Same card chrome, the tinted channel-header band with monospace P · E · S letters, and the channel-triad trailing slot: three 22×22 chips per row. Two sub-grouped cards under one overline — "Notify me" (with a host reminder lead-time chip row) and "Notify attendees" (booking confirmation locked on). The SMS column is present but locked with a coming-soon treatment. Scheduling rows accent in the active pillar.
