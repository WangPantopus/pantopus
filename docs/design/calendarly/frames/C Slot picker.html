Pick a time

The full-screen slot picker an invitee reaches after choosing an event type (or via a single-type deep link, or the reschedule CTA on Manage your booking). The month calendar and the day's slots stack in one scroll — not a wizard split. It mirrors the Support Train weekday/time grid and A10.9 slot rows over Home calendar's month strip. Every day is a real, labelled button; available days are solid, unavailable muted, today carries a pillar ring, and the selected day is a filled pillar circle. The accent follows the host's pillar (Personal sky here) for today and selected; all controls stay product sky.
